- A full-width title bar and floating player, with separate floating navigation

  reloads the open playlist, and older requests cannot overwrite newer results.

  along with macOS traffic-light spacing and Windows caption buttons.

**Migrating from Cherry 1.x?** Keep the old app installed and close it before
importing. After installing this version, choose **Migrate from 1.x** on the
welcome screen after accepting the disclaimer. Verify your imported data before
uninstalling the old app.
