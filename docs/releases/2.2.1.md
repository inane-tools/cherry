Cherry 2.2.1 **Nemuro** fixes playback and playlist bugs.

- The **sleep timer** pauses the song that is actually playing when the track changes during its fade-out, and a song still loading when the fade ends stays paused.
- **Previous** restarts a restored song instead of skipping back to the one before it.
- **Last.fm** scrobbles of a restored song are dated from when playback starts, not from when the app opened.
- A **playlist or album that only partly loads** is no longer remembered as the whole list, and its song count is no longer lowered to match.
- A **song removed from a playlist** no longer reappears, or plays, when the playlist is refreshed or reopened right away.
