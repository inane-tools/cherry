Cherry 2.3.0 **Nayoro** refreshes Settings and the app's surfaces, and adds two experimental backgrounds.

- **Settings** is wider and opens as one page with section chips that jump to each section. Player now sits under Appearance. Integrations, keyboard shortcuts and the open-source list start collapsed.
- **Appearance** shows its choices as previews: theme thumbnails, the current song's artwork for song-art accents, and both sidebar layouts.
- **Glass surfaces**: the sidebars, player bar, popovers, menus and dialogs share one blurred, tinted surface, and the player bar keeps its accent gradient. Settings and the playlist sheets stay opaque.
- **Account** moved from the top bar to its own panel at the bottom of the sidebar. When an update is available, the avatar gets a yellow badge and the account menu lists the update.
- **Player**: the scrubber sits on a darker inset, and the expanded player dims the whole window.
- **Compact sidebar**: the back button and page title line up with the sidebar and the page content.
- **Experimental music visualizer** (Settings → Player): accent-coloured shapes behind the window react to the music.
- **Experimental music video background** (Settings → Player): in video mode, the video plays dimmed behind the window while the player is collapsed.
