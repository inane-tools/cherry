Cherry 2.3.1 **Nayoro** fixes scrobbling, the tray, the saved queue, sign-in and playlist removals.

- **Last.fm** counts real listening time, so long songs played in a hidden or muted window are scrobbled.
- Clicking the **tray icon**, or Show Cherry in its menu, brings back a minimized window.
- A **damaged saved queue order** is rebuilt on launch instead of skipping or repeating songs.
- **Cancelling YouTube sign-in** while a status check is running no longer finishes the sign-in or leaves a stale message.
- A **song removed from a playlist** no longer comes back after a reload when the playlist was loaded from an older cache.
